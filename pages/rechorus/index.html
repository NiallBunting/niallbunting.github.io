How to use Rechorus

1. Setup
  • Load an MP3 backing track.
  • Wear headphones to prevent feedback!
  • Turn up Monitor to hear yourself in real-time.
  • Align the Green and Blue delays by playing back the audio into the microphone.
2. Control
  • Drag on waveform to loop a specific section.
  • Double-click waveform to clear selection.
  • Blue Sync: Adjust if your recorded take sounds late/early.
Keyboard Shortcuts: [Space] to Play/Stop. [R] to Stop After Loop finishes.
